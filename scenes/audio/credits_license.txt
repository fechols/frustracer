Ambience loops for the world islands (src/audio.rs::AMBIENCE).

All source recordings are licensed CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain
dedication — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), verified
per sound on its freesound.org page at download time (2026-07-22).
Attribution is not required by CC0; it is given anyway, with thanks.
helmet.ogg is the exception: fully synthesized in-repo (no source
recording), dedicated CC0 the same way.

Processing applied here (identical per file, see the scene-acquisition notes
in CLAUDE.md and src/audio.rs): trimmed to a ~48 s bed, downmixed to mono,
resampled to 48 kHz, re-encoded Ogg Vorbis q3 with ffmpeg. Loudness is
normalized and the loop seam crossfaded at LOAD time by the renderer
(audio.rs), not baked into the files.
