Changes made in this repository, for the record:
  * the OBJ meshes are stored zstd-compressed (.obj.zst); the loader
    decodes them transparently and a plain .obj still loads
  * PNG textures were converted to LOSSLESS WebP (bit-identical RGBA;
    the manifests keep their .png names and the loader falls back to
    the .webp sibling). JPEGs were left as JPEG.
No geometry, material or texel values were altered.

Only the Exterior scene is used by the default world; Interior ships
too and is loadable as a scene argument.
