TEXTURES (rungholt-*.webp, house-*.webp): the texture atlases in this
directory are NOT the ones from the original conversion. The original
Mineways export embedded Mojang's default Minecraft block textures, which
may not be redistributed. They have been rebuilt cell-for-cell (same
Mineways atlas layout, so the OBJ UVs are unchanged) from:

  Pixel Perfection, by Hugh "XSSheep" Rutland and contributors
  (Community Edition continuation: https://github.com/Athemis/PixelPerfectionCE;
   Minetest port used for a few tiles: https://github.com/minetest-texture-packs/Pixel-Perfection)
  License: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

A handful of blocks with no Pixel Perfection equivalent (redstone wire,
repeaters, pistons, melon, portal, nether wart, jack-o-lantern face,
enchantment-table top, jukebox top) are simple original drawings or
recompositions of Pixel Perfection art made for this repository. Flat
single-color material swatch cells and per-cell average-color fills are
retained/derived as uncopyrightable flat colors. Biome-tinted foliage
tiles carry constant color multipliers.

The derived atlas images are themselves licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
