Changes made in this repository, for the record:
  * the OBJ mesh is stored zstd-compressed (.obj.zst); the loader
    decodes it transparently and a plain .obj still loads
  * PNG textures were converted to LOSSLESS WebP (bit-identical RGBA;
    the manifest keeps its .png names and the loader falls back to the
    .webp sibling)
  * the texture atlas (vokselia_spawn.webp) was REBUILT cell-for-cell:
    the original Mineways export embedded Mojang's default Minecraft
    block textures, which may not be redistributed. Replacement art is
    from Pixel Perfection by Hugh "XSSheep" Rutland and contributors
    (CC BY-SA 4.0; Community Edition:
    https://github.com/Athemis/PixelPerfectionCE, Minetest port for a
    few tiles: https://github.com/minetest-texture-packs/Pixel-Perfection),
    plus simple original drawings for blocks with no Pixel Perfection
    equivalent, flat average-color fills for unreferenced cells, and
    constant tint multipliers on biome-colored foliage. The atlas
    LAYOUT (and therefore the OBJ UVs) is unchanged.
    The derived atlas image is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; credit
    "Pixel Perfection by XSSheep and contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0".
No geometry or material values were altered.
