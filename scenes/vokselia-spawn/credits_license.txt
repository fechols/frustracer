The spawn town of the Vokselia Minecraft world, converted to OBJ for the
McGuire Computer Graphics Archive:
  https://casual-effects.com/data/

Vokselia, CC BY 3.0" in any use or redistribution.

CC BY 3.0 permits redistribution, including commercially, provided
attribution is given and changes are indicated.

Changes made in this repository, for the record:
  * the OBJ mesh is stored zstd-compressed (.obj.zst); the loader
    decodes it transparently and a plain .obj still loads
  * PNG textures were converted to LOSSLESS WebP (bit-identical RGBA;
    the manifest keeps its .png names and the loader falls back to the
    .webp sibling)
No geometry, material or texel values were altered.
