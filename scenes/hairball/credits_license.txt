Changes made in this repository, for the record: the OBJ mesh is stored
zstd-compressed (.obj.zst); the loader decodes it transparently and a
plain .obj still loads. No geometry or material values were altered.

Not part of the default world -- world.rs's CURATED table skips it
deliberately (pathological BVH depth: it is the traversal-stack margin
stress case, not a place). Loadable as a scene argument.
