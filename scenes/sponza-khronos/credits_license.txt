Changes made in this repository, for the record:
  * external .bin buffers are stored zstd-compressed (.bin.zst); the
    loader decodes them transparently and a plain .bin still loads
  * three PNG textures were converted to LOSSLESS WebP (bit-identical
    RGBA; the .gltf keeps its .png URIs and the loader falls back to
    the .webp sibling). The 68 JPEG textures were left as JPEG.
No geometry, material or texel values were altered.
